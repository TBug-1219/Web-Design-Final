About Me

My name is Victoria Buckner. I am 19 years old. I was born in Kansas City and raised in Belton. I started dancing at the age 7 at the Movement & Company Dance and performing arts. I danced there through the end of my senior year and now teach dance there. I academically excelled in high School and even took enough classes to be considered a sophomore when I started college here at Avila University. I lettered all four years of high School in dance and academics, one year in band, and another year in stuco. I have academic recognition from state as well as elementary, junior, highschool, and college honor societies. I am now a computer science major and an Avila Glitter Girl. Last year I became a national champion at the college level and hope to defend that title this year in April.
