Game Day

So far this season we have been dancing at the home football games. Outside games, while the weather conditions might be all over the place, are some of my favorite games. Being on Avila's dance team has been a dream of mine for as long as I can remember The program is something I really enjoy. This year we had a ring ceremony to present us with the rings we won at nationals the year before.

Competition

Last Year we competed both a lyrical/contemporary dance and a hiphop dance. We took home a second place by 1.3 points for hiphip. We took home a national championship in lyrical/contemporary by 2.12 points. That performance earned the highest score in Avila dance history. This was my first time dancing on the college national floor and it was a completely different experience than I have ever experienced before. It is exhilarating and something that is indescribable.
